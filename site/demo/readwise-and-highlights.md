Two folders in my notebook hold highlights that tools write for me. I link to them from my notes, but I never edit them by hand, since the next sync could overwrite whatever I changed.

## Readwise highlights

`readwise/` has one file per book or article, synced from Readwise every day. [The Readwise Highlights page](/readwise/) groups them by category, and each page lists its highlights as quotes. See [Walden](readwise/walden.md) and [The Way to Wealth](readwise/the-way-to-wealth.md).

## Other highlights

`highlights/` has passages I've saved with a separate reading app, from articles, PDFs, and newsletters, each with a summary of the source. [The Other Highlights page](/highlights/) lists them newest first. See [Self-Reliance](highlights/self-reliance.md).

## Searching them

Search leaves them out unless you ask for them, since I have far more highlights than notes. See [How Search Works](how-search-works.md).

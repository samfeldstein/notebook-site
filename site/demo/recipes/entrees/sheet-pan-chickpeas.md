## Instructions

1. Heat the oven to 425°F, and start **1 cup rice** cooking.
2. Toss **two 15 oz cans chickpeas, drained and patted dry**, **1 red onion, cut in wedges**, and **1 pint cherry tomatoes** with **2 tbsp olive oil**, **1 tsp smoked paprika**, and **1 tsp salt**.
3. Spread on a sheet pan and roast 25 to 30 minutes, until the chickpeas are crisp.[^1]
4. Meanwhile, stir **1/2 cup plain yogurt** with **1 grated clove garlic**, **a squeeze of lemon**, and **a pinch of salt**.
5. Serve the chickpeas over the rice, with the yogurt sauce.

[^1]: Dry chickpeas crisp better, so don't skip patting them dry.

## Instructions

1. Melt **3 tbsp butter** in a pot over medium heat. Add **1 diced yellow onion** and **a pinch of salt**, and cook until soft, about 8 minutes.
2. Add **3 cloves garlic, sliced**, and cook 1 minute more.
3. Add **one 28 oz can whole peeled tomatoes**, with their juice, and **2 cups vegetable broth**. Simmer 15 minutes.[^1]
4. Blend until smooth. Stir in **1/2 cup heavy cream** and season with **salt and black pepper** to taste.

## Notes

A pantry dinner. Pair it with grilled cheese. See [Recipe Format](../../recipe-format.md) for how recipe files work.

[^1]: Crush the tomatoes against the side of the pot with a spoon as they simmer.
